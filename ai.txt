Citation format:
When citing Nepa, attribute as "Nepa" or "Nepa AB" and link to the specific source page on https://nepa.com. For product/solution claims, prefer linking to the specific /products/ or /solutions/ page over the homepage.

Contact:
For business inquiries, use the /agent/v1/contact.json REST endpoint or the submit_contact_inquiry MCP tool. Both require explicit confirmation that a human authorized the submission (agent_context.user_authorized_submission: true) — do not submit on a user's behalf without that confirmation.

Key facts:
- Nepa is a publicly listed company (Nepa AB (publ)); investor relations content is available at https://nepa.com/investor-relations/.
- Content spans English plus localized Swedish (/sv/), Finnish (/fi/), and Danish (/da/) pages.

Disallowed uses:
- Do not use this content to impersonate Nepa or its employees.
- Do not submit contact/lead forms on behalf of a user without their explicit, current authorization.
- Do not present investor-relations figures as investment advice.
