Continuously updated marketing mix modelling that quantifies the short-term sales impact and long-term brand-building effect of each marketing channel and campaign. https://nepa.com/products/marketing-mix-modeling/

A rapid campaign evaluation solution that measures whether campaigns are noticed, understood, linked to the brand, and how they affect purchase intent, benchmarked against a database of more than 2,800 campaigns. Comparisons

Continuous tracking vs. ad hoc analysis

Brand Tracking is a continuously updated dashboard — always-on, refreshed against real-time data. Category Insight is an ad hoc, one-off market analysis of category structure and competitive positioning, delivered within weeks. The two are complementary: Category Insight is often used to set initial strategic direction (e.g. the Dafgårds and Fortnox cases), while Brand Tracking measures performance against that direction on an ongoing basis.

Nepa Trinity vs. individual products

Nepa Trinity bundles three existing products — Marketing Mix Modelling, Brand Tracking, and Campaign Pulse — into one AI-supported decision framework for brands investing 20M+ per year in media. It is not a separate product; a brand can instead buy any of the three individually (see Products) without the unified Trinity framework and its board-level reporting layer.

FAQ

What makes Nepa's Brand Tracking different from traditional solutions?

Unlike traditional trackers that deliver data weeks later, Nepa provides real-time brand health insights powered by AI-enhanced analysis. This means you can act faster, spot risks earlier, and make smarter marketing decisions—without waiting for static reports.

How does AI improve my brand tracking?

Our AI models continuously scan your brand data to detect trends, highlight risks and opportunities, and link campaigns to brand outcomes. You'll see changes as they happen—not weeks later—so you can adapt strategy in real time.

What kind of brand metrics can I track with Nepa?

You can track brand awareness, perception, loyalty, and campaign impact. Add-ons like Category Entry Points, BrandPremium, and Brand Touch let you go even deeper—quantifying brand equity, mental availability, and emotional positioning.

Can I customize what I see in the dashboards?

Yes. From onboarding, we tailor your dashboards around your KPIs, filters, and audiences. You'll get clear, easy-to-use views that deliver clarity, not clutter, and support faster decision-making.

What kind of support will I get after going live?

You'll have a dedicated team of experts monitoring your data, flagging important shifts, and guiding actions—no ticket system needed. We also hold regular insight meetings to turn data into strategy and help you prove ROI.

Who is Nepa's Brand Tracking best suited for?

It's ideal for marketing leaders who need to prove brand impact quickly, optimize spend, and connect efforts directly to growth. Especially valuable for brands seeking to move faster and stay ahead of shifting markets.

How do I get started?

Just book a demo. In less than an hour, we'll show you how smarter tracking can elevate your strategy and accelerate brand growth—starting today.

How often is brand tracking data updated?

Brand data is collected continuously and refreshed weekly, giving you near real-time visibility into awareness, perception, and campaign effectiveness.

Can I track multiple brands or markets at the same time?

Yes. You can monitor multiple brands, competitors, or regions within one dashboard and compare results across audiences and campaigns.

How much does Nepa's Brand Tracking platform cost?

Pricing starts at 15,000 SEK/month, including brand funnel tracking, 8 brands, 8 target groups, and unlimited logins. Custom plans are available for larger needs.

Note (crawl 2026-10-16): this is the answer as published on the product page. The pricing page widget lists Brand Tracking Mini from 1,500 EUR (16,300 SEK)/month — see #pricing.

Can Nepa's Brand Tracking connect with my marketing or CRM data?

Yes. Nepa's platform integrates with marketing analytics and CRM systems, allowing you to link brand performance directly to campaign and sales results.

How long does it take to set up brand tracking?

Most clients go live within a few weeks. Our team handles onboarding, data setup, and dashboard configuration so you can start tracking insights quickly.

What industries does Nepa's Brand Tracking work best for?

Nepa works across industries including FMCG, retail, finance, and tech. The platform adapts to your specific KPIs and competitive landscape.

Can I benchmark my brand against competitors?

Absolutely. Nepa's dashboards allow you to compare your brand performance against competitors across awareness, perception, and loyalty metrics.
